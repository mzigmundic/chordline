# Chordline

A backing track builder for guitarists. Put chords on a timeline, pick instruments and a drum groove, and play along.

**Open it:** https://mzigmundic.github.io/chordline/

- Chords: 19 types in every key, plus your own chords built on a keyboard, with name suggestions
- Progressions: 18 common ones (pop, rock, blues, minor and metal) in any key, and your own saved progressions
- Timeline: drag chords in, drag the right edge to change length, any of 7 time signatures, 40–220 bpm, count-in
- Sound: 18 sampled instruments and 3 sampled basses (FluidR3 GM soundfont), synth pad and pluck, 37 rock and metal drum styles
- Installable: on Android, open the page in Chrome and choose *Install app* / *Add to home screen*. Instruments you have used work offline.

Your chords, progressions and settings are stored in your browser on each device.

Credits: FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen (CC BY 3.0), via the MIDI.js soundfont builds on npm. Fonts: Marcellus, Figtree and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License), via Fontsource.
